# Methodology

This tracker uses separate scores instead of one good/bad rating.

## Source Grades

- A: Government filing, city agenda, utility/ACC document
- B: Major news source or official organization release
- C: Local reporting or public-meeting material
- D: Petition, social media, or community-only claim
- E: Unverified / needs confirmation

## Planning Complexity Formula

```text
powerDemandComplexity * 0.28
+ communitySensitivity * 0.22
+ policyMomentum * 0.18
+ inverse infrastructureReadiness * 0.12
+ inverse waterPlanningStrength * 0.12
+ inverse transparency * 0.08
```

## Balanced Opportunity Formula

```text
economicUpside * 0.32
+ infrastructureReadiness * 0.22
+ transparency * 0.18
+ waterPlanningStrength * 0.12
+ policyMomentum * 0.10
+ inverse communitySensitivity * 0.06
```

The formulas are intentionally simple so users can understand and challenge them.
